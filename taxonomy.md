# Categories

Every article is filed under exactly one primary `category` (its slug is used in
`index.json`) and may carry any number of free-form `tags`. Keep the category
set small and stable; prefer adding tags over inventing new categories.

| Slug | Name | Covers |
|------|------|--------|
| `personalization-recsys` | Personalization & Recommender Systems | Ranking, retrieval, candidate generation, cold-start, artwork/asset personalization, two-tower and sequence models |
| `llm-genai` | LLMs & Generative AI | Foundation models, fine-tuning, RAG, agents, prompt/context engineering, generative recommendation |
| `ml-infra-serving` | ML Infrastructure & Serving | Model serving, inference engines, GPU scheduling, feature stores, training platforms, MLOps |
| `data-engineering` | Data Engineering | Pipelines, streaming, lakehouse, feature freshness, knowledge graphs, data quality |
| `search-ranking` | Search & Ranking | Query understanding, semantic/embedding retrieval, generative retrieval, learning-to-rank |
| `experimentation-causal` | Experimentation & Causal Inference | A/B testing, interleaving, uplift modelling, causal inference, metrics design |
| `cv-multimodal` | Computer Vision & Multimodal | Image/video/audio understanding, multimodal embeddings, media ML |
| `forecasting-timeseries` | Forecasting & Time Series | Demand forecasting, anomaly detection, temporal models |
| `research-foundational` | Research & Foundational | Methods-first papers and results not tied to one product surface |

## Novelty scale (used by Claude's Take)

- **5 — Field-shifting.** Introduces a genuinely new paradigm or result others will copy.
- **4 — Strong.** A notable advance or a clever production-first take on a hot idea.
- **3 — Solid.** Good engineering; incremental over known techniques, well executed.
- **2 — Incremental.** Useful write-up, little that is new.
- **1 — Recap.** Mostly a survey or restatement of established practice.
