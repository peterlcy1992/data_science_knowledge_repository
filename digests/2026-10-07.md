## 🔬 Deep Dive: Token-Budgeted Escalation for Financial Document QA

**Source:** [arXiv (Junru Zhu, Yixin Yang, Xiaoqing Ding, Ruoyu Qi)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07760) · Published 2026-10 · Category: LLMs & GenAI (AI/ML, DS-flavoured cost/benefit decisions)

*(AI/ML-systems was due in the rotation: the last 4 deep dives were 2 Core DS, 2 AI/ML.)*

**Business.** RAG systems can answer hard questions better by escalating to more retrieval, but tokens cost money. Which queries deserve the extra spend under a fixed budget?

**Technical.** On 150 FinanceBench questions: answer with top-1 retrieval, optionally escalate to top-5, ranking escalation candidates by predicted benefit-to-cost ratio. Extra-call cost is predictable (R² 0.93); which queries benefit is not (AUROC 0.60).

**Impact.** At a 10% token budget, gain-per-token prioritization improved answer quality by 0.034 points using 46.6% fewer tokens than uniform top-5. Small benchmark; full paper not read.

**Claude's Take (3/5).** Uplift-targeting applied to LLM calls. The useful lesson is that the bottleneck is the benefit (uplift) model, not the cost model.
