## 🔎 Deep dive: Selling in the Wild: Benchmark and Randomized Field Evidence for a Sales Agent Trained on Commercial Conversations

*arXiv cs.IR (Tannenbaum), Oct 2026 · llm-genai · novelty 4/5*
Why this pick: AI/ML-systems was due (2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS), and this is the strongest AI entry with a Core-DS flavour: a randomized, guardrailed field experiment.

🎙️ Deep-dive podcast: podcasts/2026-10-20-arxiv-selling-in-the-wild-sales-agent-field-rct.m4a
🎙️ Episode S1E42 — cover ✓, RSS draft ✓ (unpublished; publish from the RSS.com dashboard)

**Business context.** Conversational sales agents are widely deployed, but evidence is mostly benchmark scores or vendor claims. The paper asks the commercial question directly: does a purpose-trained agent outsell no agent, a general frontier-model agent in the same interface, and the site's own human staff?

**How it works.** Javo, fine-tuned on 4.6M consented commercial conversations, was scored on two public sales benchmarks and then tested in an RCT on 100 live websites across 14 industries (1 May–30 Sep 2026), with about 14.8M visitors randomized to no agent, Javo, or a frontier-model agent with the same interface, tools and guardrails. On 20 sites with staffed live chat it was compared with human associates using a prespecified −7% non-inferiority margin. Method detail is abstract-level.

**Impact (as reported).** Revenue per visitor +10.0% vs no agent (95% CI +5.1 to +14.9); engaged-visitor conversion +21.6% vs the frontier agent (95% CI +16.9 to +26.3); engaged conversion 11.26% vs 11.20% for humans (non-inferior, p = 0.004; not shown superior) at 1.8% of the cost per conversation; return-rate, unsupported-claim and complaint guardrails met. Unclear: definition of "engaged", cost accounting, and the author's relationship to Javo.

**Claude's Take.** Randomized, large-scale, prespecified guardrails and a non-inferiority design are rare in LLM-agent papers; the design is the contribution. Caveats: single author, abstract-level read, and engaged-visitor analyses condition on a post-randomization variable.
