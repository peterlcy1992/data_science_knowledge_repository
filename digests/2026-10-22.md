## 🔎 Deep dive: From Prompts to Posteriors: Can LLM Agents Perform Bayesian Inference in Context?

*arXiv cs.AI (Filbry, Melo & Gal), Oct 2026 · llm-genai · novelty 4/5*
Why this pick: AI/ML-systems was due (2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS), and this is an AI piece that ties directly to DS practice: inference quality of agents used for hypothesis generation and experiment design.

🎙️ Deep-dive podcast: podcasts/2026-10-22-arxiv-prompts-to-posteriors-llm-bayesian-inference.m4a

**Business context.** LLM agents are used for literature review, hypothesis generation and experiment design, all of which need continual belief updating, which Bayesian inference formalizes. Prior work assumed LLMs can't do principled in-context updating and wrapped them in heuristics.

**How it works.** Three-part evaluation: qualitative epistemological examples probing probabilistic coherence, quantitative Bayesian-inference benchmarks (internal consistency and recovery of an externally determined posterior), and analysis of how non-Bayesian updates affect downstream tasks. Models, datasets and metrics are not in the abstract.

**Impact (as reported).** Without tools, LLMs generally violate Bayes' rule in context; with modern reasoning plus code execution, performance improves substantially, even approximating analytically intractable posteriors. The authors argue tool-equipped LLMs could already support complex belief updates in science.

**Claude's Take.** Novelty 4/5: a testable DS-relevant question, and "tools rescue what raw reasoning lacks" is practical. Abstract-level read; no model names or numbers visible.
