## 🔬 Deep Dive: Sequentially-Rerandomized Switchback Experiments

**Source:** [arXiv (Zeng, Adjaho, Bucarey, Qin, Zhang, Hoban, Johari, Wager)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02489) · Published 2026-04 · Category: Experimentation & Causal

*(Core DS was due in the rotation: only 1 of the last 4 deep dives was Core DS.)*

**Business.** Platforms and marketplaces often can't randomize users, so they randomize operational units (regions, clusters) over time. The paper names the pain points: few units, cross-unit heterogeneity, non-stationarity and carryover.

**Technical.** SRSB re-randomizes treatment at every time step while keeping assignment balanced on predetermined variables derived from history. It comes with finite-sample and asymptotic inference, plus a blocked variant that rerandomizes within strata defined by the previous treatment ("stay" groups) to handle carryover.

**Impact.** Per the abstract, simulations show improvements and robustness over traditional switchbacks. No production deployment or effect sizes are reported (full paper not read).

**Claude's Take (4/5).** A design-stage fix rather than another estimator, and it complements covariate-adjustment (CUPED-style) analysis. Evidence is simulation-only so far.
