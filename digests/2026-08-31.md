## Deep dive: From Models to Products — LLMs for Recommendation at Spotify Scale

**Source:** [Spotify Research](https://research.atspotify.com/2026/8/from-models-to-products-llms-for-recommendation-at-spotify-scale) · Category: LLMs & Generative AI · Novelty **4/5**

**Why it's worth your time:** Spotify moved LLM-based recommendation from a
research idea to a live product surface — with a 21-day A/B test at ~20M
impressions per cell to show for it.

### Business context

LLMs are appealing for recommendation because they can reason jointly over
language, user context, and catalog structure, and can in principle be
steered with natural-language instructions instead of retraining. The
practical blocker is **grounding**: ask an LLM to recommend from a huge,
shifting catalog and it will happily hallucinate items that don't exist.
Spotify's goal was to get past that blocker and into production — specifically
to surface podcast episodes listeners wouldn't otherwise find through their
existing habits.

### Technical details

- **NEO**, the general framework, treats recommendation as one case of a
  broader problem: adapting LLMs to reason over language, users, and
  structured domain entities — a template Spotify frames as reusable beyond
  recommendation.
- **GLIDE**, the production system, is built on a **compact 1B-parameter LLM**
  and formulates recommendation as **instruction-following over a discretized
  catalog**, solved via **Semantic IDs** so every output the model generates
  maps to a real catalog item — no hallucinated titles.
- GLIDE conditions on recent listening history and lightweight context, and
  injects **long-term user embeddings as soft prompts** to keep stable
  preferences legible under tight latency budgets.
- Rather than replacing the existing stack, GLIDE plugs in as an **additional
  candidate generator** feeding the existing downstream ranking pipeline — a
  low-risk way to get a still-maturing LLM component into production.

### Impact — potential & realized

- **Realized:** in a 21-day online A/B test on Spotify Home (~20M impressions
  per cell), GLIDE lifted **non-habitual podcast listening by 5.4%** and
  **new-show discovery by 14.3%**, while holding latency and engagement
  guardrails.
- **Potential:** NEO's grounding + steerability recipe is pitched as a
  template for other large-scale discovery/retrieval problems, not just
  recommendation.

### Claude's Take

**Novelty 4/5.** Plenty of research proposes LLM-based recommendation; a live
A/B test with real traffic and a genuine lift on a metric as hard to move as
new-show discovery is comparatively rare. The recipe — semantic IDs for
grounding, a small dedicated LLM, soft-prompted long-term preferences — is
production-pragmatic rather than fundamentally new, hence 4 and not 5.

**Similar work:** Spotify's own semantic-ID generative retrieval for podcasts
and its adaptive-decoding (SPDD) technique (both in this bank); Netflix's
GenRec, a different production LLM-native recommender.

**Jargon:** _grounding_ = constraining generation to real, valid items instead
of free text; _semantic ID_ = a short discrete code sequence standing in for
an item; _soft prompt_ = a learned vector injected into the model's input in
place of natural-language tokens.
