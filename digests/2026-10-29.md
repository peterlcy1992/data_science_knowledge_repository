## 🔎 Deep dive: Exploratory creative pre-ranking: Hierarchical empirical-Bayes Thompson sampling for real-time creative optimization

*arXiv (UCLA Statistics & Data Science with Roblox) · personalization-recsys · AI/ML pick (rotation: 2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS, so AI/ML-systems was due; most recently added AI/ML entry with novelty ≥ 3 that is statistics-forward) · novelty 3/5 · full text read*

**The problem.** Generative AI has made ad creatives nearly free, so creative pools outgrow ad pools while feedback per creative stays sparse and the latency budget is tight. Prior industrial systems that select creatives before ranking use deterministic point predictors with no calibrated uncertainty, so fresh creatives are reached only through a separate exploration mechanism.

**The method.** Creatives sit in a hierarchy (audience segment, advertised item, campaign, creative). A cold creative borrows from its parents through a Beta-Bernoulli empirical-Bayes cascade whose shrinkage strength is set in closed form from historical logs (one parameter per level, nothing tuned). To fit the latency budget, it predicts for an audience segment rather than a user, rebuilding the segment posterior from dense single-attribute counters combined on the log-odds scale with one fitted weight per attribute. Selection is one Thompson draw per creative; campaigns get shortlist shares proportional to pool size, filled by round robin so extra uploads buy no extra exposure.

**Results.** Replay of logged Roblox Sponsored-ads requests (rewards are the deployed ranker's predicted play-through rate, 95% request-clustered bootstrap): +8.14% (CI 7.64 to 8.65) vs +2.80% for an equally sized random shortlist, more than 40% fewer creatives forwarded than the full pool, and +0.2% serving time (about 41.6 ms vs 41.5 ms). It recovers 44% of the gain available from forwarding every creative. A second deployed ranker preserved the method ordering. The paper says it now serves in production after an A/B test on live traffic, but the text gives no live numbers.

**Claude's Take (opinion).** The reusable idea is the tuning-free, closed-form shrinkage strength for hierarchical cold-start estimates plus a single Thompson draw to buy exploration almost for free. The evidence is a replay on predicted rewards from a few hundred requests, so the real gain rests on the unquantified live test.
