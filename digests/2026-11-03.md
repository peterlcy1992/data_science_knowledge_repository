## 🔎 Deep dive: How Much Fresh Data Should CTR Recalibration Use? A Temporal Evaluation of Post-hoc Calibration

*arXiv cs.LG (Rui Liu, independent researcher) · personalization-recsys (AI/ML-systems) · AI/ML-systems pick (rotation: 2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS, so AI/ML-systems was due) · novelty 3/5 · full text read*

**The problem.** A CTR model's output is consumed as a probability by bidding and pacing, but offline CTR evaluation mostly uses random splits with AUC and log loss, and post-hoc recalibration (Platt scaling, isotonic regression) is often run unconditionally on every refresh.

**The setup.** Avazu, 40.4M impressions over ten days (CTR 16.98%); logistic regression, DeepFM and DCN-v2; three seeds; two rolling temporal origins plus a random-split arm. Each test day is recalibrated using a leak-free slice of the preceding hours; calibration is measured by ECE with paired block-bootstrap intervals.

**Findings.**
- Random splits flatter calibration: LR's ECE is 55–62% of its temporal value in every seed (the deep models' ratio is seed-unstable); random-split AUC is also higher by about +0.014.
- ECE is not monotone in distance from training for any model.
- Recalibrating on a 6-hour slice removes up to 61% of ECE in badly miscalibrated cells, but raises ECE in 14–17 of 48 applications across seeds.
- On fixed evaluation sets a 12-hour slice cuts harm from 13–17 to 3–5 of 36 applications; 24 hours raises it again. A 12-hour slice subsampled to the 1-hour slice's size recovers 88% of the gain and is 20–25× faster to fit, so impression count alone does not explain it.
- A pre-serving slice-ECE gate shows no benefit out-of-origin; in a simulated auction recalibration moves profit only about −0.21% to +0.26%.

**Caveats.** One public dataset from 2014, ten days; the author says the finding that a wide span beat the freshest few hours needs confirmation on other logs.

**Claude's Take (opinion).** A modest, honest measurement study; the useful part is the protocol checklist (evaluate calibration temporally, over multiple origins and seeds, don't recalibrate unconditionally), not a new method.
