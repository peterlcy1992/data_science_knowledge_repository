## 🔬 Deep Dive: Agentic RAG Evaluation: Budget Allocation Across Questions, Trajectories, and Reads

**Source:** [arXiv (Jingjie Ning, Xueqi Li, Yibo Kong)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05034) · Published 2026-10 · Category: LLMs & GenAI (AI/ML, DS-flavoured evaluation)

*(AI/ML-systems was due in the rotation: the last 4 deep dives were 2 Core DS, 2 AI/ML.)*

**Business.** Agentic RAG evals are noisy: agents take different search trajectories and judges read them differently. Teams with a fixed budget have little guidance on buying more questions versus more repeats.

**Technical.** Generalizability theory plus repeated sampling on HotpotQA and MuSiQue decomposes evaluation variance into question, trajectory and read components, then predicts the standard error of alternative allocations (3.5-4.0% prediction accuracy reported).

**Impact.** At about 34M tokens, broader question coverage lowers standard error by 33% vs. five reads and 12.6% vs. three trajectories. Temperature 0 cut answer disagreement from 14.3% to 3.4%. Two benchmarks only; full paper not read.

**Claude's Take (4/5).** A statistician's answer to an LLM-eval question: the same variance-components logic as clustered A/B power analysis. "More questions beats more repeats" is intuitive; quantifying it is what makes it actionable.
