## 🔬 Deep Dive: A/B Agent — A Self-Evolving Agent for Strategy Iteration in Industrial A/B Testing

**Source:** [arXiv (Jiang et al., Kuaishou E-commerce)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04625) · Published 2026-08 · Category: LLMs & Generative AI

*(AI/ML-systems was due in the rotation: 2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS.)*

**Business.** Recommender teams iterate by running many A/B tests on strategies, but choosing the next one depends on scarce expert intuition and knowledge scattered across hundreds of past experiments.

**Technical.** Past strategies are organised in a hierarchical experience tree (scenario → stage → objective). Multi-path Tree-RAG retrieval generates new actionable strategies, and continuous A/B feedback drives parameter tuning and knowledge updates. The authors built a benchmark from 310 historical strategies across three Kuaishou E-commerce scenarios.

**Impact.** Author-reported +4.829% GMV on a short-video e-commerce platform with guardrail metrics positive; experiment-design detail (duration, CIs, multiple-testing handling) was not available.

**Claude's Take (4/5).** Using the A/B log itself as an agent's corpus is the measurement-aware angle the agent-for-recsys wave lacks — but an agent running many tests creates a winner's-curse problem, so the headline lift needs holdout validation.
