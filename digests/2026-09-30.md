## 🔬 Deep Dive: Adaptive Instructed-Retriever: Frontier-Quality Search at 2x Lower Latency

**Source:** [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/adaptive-instructed-retriever-frontier-quality-search-2x-lower-latency) · Published 2026-09 · Category: Search & Ranking

*(AI/ML-systems was due in the rotation: 2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS.)*

**Business.** Enterprise data agents need both accuracy and speed. Single-step retrieval is fast but weak on multi-hop questions; sequential multi-step search is accurate but slow.

**Technical.** Databricks trains a retrieval agent with online RL using CISPO (Clipped Importance Sampling Policy Optimization) to learn when to keep searching versus return early. It reuses Instructed-Retriever-1 training data for fast parallel search, adds synthetic multi-hop questions, and uses a reward balancing trajectory quality against search cost. Sequential steps have a fixed upper bound, so latency is bounded, and training yields a Pareto frontier of checkpoints for tuning the quality–latency trade-off.

**Impact.** Databricks reports a 5.8 s average end-to-end response time, over 2x faster than several frontier models while matching leading third-party models on retrieval benchmarks. These are vendor-run numbers; treat as directional.

**Claude's Take (3/5).** A clean example of learning a stopping rule for agentic retrieval; the Pareto-of-checkpoints framing is practical, but the comparison is vendor-run and benchmark/latency details are thin.
