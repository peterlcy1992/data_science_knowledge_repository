## 🔬 Deep Dive: Grab Bench: Evaluating AI on Grab-shaped production work

**Source:** [Grab Engineering Blog](https://engineering.grab.com/grab-bench-evaluating-ai) · Published 2026-09 · Category: LLMs & Generative AI

*(AI/ML-systems was due in the rotation: 2 of the last 4 deep dives were Core DS.)*

Grab's worry was not blatant hallucination but *plausibility*: valid-looking SQL, tool calls and reasoning that quietly break a contract or miss a hidden requirement. Public leaderboards don't surface those failures on Grab's own work, so Grab built an internal harness.

**Design.** Grab Bench is a configurable harness with task-specific plugins; each task owns its scoring contract, and one row is recorded per case/model pair. Three choices: (1) synthetic-but-realistic cases (distracted contexts, ambiguous evidence, boundary conditions) that expose no production data; (2) deterministic, contract-based scorers (ontology validation, tool parameters, test results) instead of LLM judges where the task allows; (3) explicit detection of shortcut gaming, such as fabricated evidence IDs, cite-everything patterns and agents that only pass visible tests.

**Findings and caveats.** More reasoning is not universally good — it can hurt tasks needing literal precision while helping planning-heavy ones. The most valuable output is the row-level failure taxonomy, not the leaderboard. Grab itself notes synthetic evaluation shows contract compliance, not production impact; live retrieval quality and user outcomes need separate evidence.

**Claude's Take (3/5).** No new algorithm, but disciplined eval design a data scientist will recognise: scorers matched to the task's contract, gaming checks, row-level analysis, and an honest caveat linking offline evals to online measurement.
