# Stanford & Marketplace Researchers — Rerandomizing Switchback Experiments

Platforms often can't randomize individual users, so they switch whole regions or markets between treatment and control over time — but few units, heterogeneity, non-stationarity and carryover make those tests noisy. This episode walks through Sequentially-Rerandomized Switchback (SRSB): treatment is re-randomized at each time step while staying balanced on covariates derived from history, with finite-sample and asymptotic inference, plus a blocked variant that rerandomizes within strata defined by the previous treatment to handle carryover. Reported evidence is simulation-based; no production results are given in the abstract.

Source article: "Sequentially-Rerandomized Switchback Experiments" — arXiv (Zeng, Adjaho, Bucarey, Qin, Zhang, Hoban, Johari, Wager), https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02489 (published 2026-04).
