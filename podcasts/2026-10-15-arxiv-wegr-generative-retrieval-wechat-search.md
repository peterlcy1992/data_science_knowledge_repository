# WeChat — Generative Retrieval for Industrial Search

A deep dive into WeGR, WeChat's unified generative-retrieval model for industrial search. We cover the business context (multi-channel retrieval stacks are costly, and generative retrieval has been held back by semantic-ID tokenizers that miss collaborative signals and collapse their codebooks, and by sparse rewards in RL fine-tuning), the technique (CoCo-VAE, a collaborative and collapse-free tokenizer, plus DHPO, which expands reward and policy-optimization horizons across the semantic-ID hierarchy without auxiliary reward models), and the reported impact: a two-month A/B test in WeChat E-commerce Search with orders up 2.539%, CTR up 1.508%, conversion up 1.381% and CPU and GPU usage down about 25%, with caveats that the abstract gives no confidence intervals or control details. Source article: "WeGR: Unified Generative Retrieval for Industrial Search Systems in WeChat" — arXiv cs.IR (WeChat), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16979 (published 2026-10).
