# Measured — When the best-fitting marketing model is the wrong one

Marketing mix models are usually chosen by out-of-sample fit, but this study shows that predictive fit can diverge from recovery of the causal quantity: channel-level return on ad spend. In 320 simulated datasets where candidates differ only in saturation form, the highest-R2 model was the RoAS-best model in just 17.5% of cases, below the 25% expected from a random pick among four. We cover why a misspecified trend term can fit better yet overstate RoAS, and how selecting models against experimental benchmarks, even noisy ones, did better. Source article: "When Predictive Fit Misleads: Selecting Marketing Mix Models Using Experimental RoAS Benchmarks" — arXiv stat.AP (Hao Chen, Alvin Lim et al., Measured Inc.), https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06111 (published 2026-11).
