# Token-Budgeted Escalation — Predicting Cost Is Easy, Predicting Benefit Is Hard

A deep dive into how to spend a limited token budget in retrieval-augmented question answering over financial documents. On 150 FinanceBench questions, the authors start with top-1 retrieval and escalate selected queries to top-5, ranking them by predicted benefit-to-cost ratio. Extra-call cost is highly predictable (R-squared 0.93), but predicting which queries actually benefit is hard (AUROC 0.60). At a 10% token budget, gain-per-token prioritization improved answer quality by 0.034 points while using 46.6% fewer tokens than uniform top-5 retrieval. Source article: "Token-Budgeted Escalation for Financial Document QA" — arXiv (Junru Zhu, Yixin Yang, Xiaoqing Ding, Ruoyu Qi), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07760 (published 2026-10).
