# AI Analysts — Keeping Type I Error Under Control as They Evolve

A deep dive into new work from Sun, Zeng and Wang on AI agents that act as analysts and keep being updated. Classical tests control Type I error for a fixed procedure, but an evolving AI analyst can drift into approving versions whose error rate exceeds the target. The authors propose continual validation: each proposed analyst version is tested on simulated null data before deployment, and they prove the lifetime probability of ever deploying a version above the allowed error level stays bounded. In two examples, Type I error reached 0.098 (bioequivalence) and 0.183 (A/B testing) without validation and at most 0.045 and 0.044 with it, while retaining 82% and 96% of the power of optimally selected valid updates. Source article: "Lifetime-valid decisions by continually evolving AI analysts" — arXiv stat.ME (Sun, Zeng & Wang), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30562 (published 2026-10).
