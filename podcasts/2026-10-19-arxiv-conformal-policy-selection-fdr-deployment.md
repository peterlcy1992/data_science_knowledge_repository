# Conformal Policy Selection — Safety Gates for Individual AI Decisions

A deep dive into Conformal Policy Selection, a statistical method for deciding where an AI targeting policy is safe to deploy for individual units rather than just on average. It frames each deployment decision as a hypothesis test, uses weighted conformal p-values that correct for the shift from the behavior policy to the target policy, and applies a selection-and-pruning step that controls the false discovery rate among selected units, with finite-sample FDR control and a doubly robust asymptotic guarantee. Synthetic experiments cover four misspecification regimes, and a semi-synthetic study on real electronic-health-record covariates shows FDR control acting as a safety gate against harmful treatments in a high-risk subpopulation. Source article: "Conformal Policy Selection: When to Deploy AI Decisions for Targeted Intervention" — arXiv stat.ME (Cao, Gao, Saar-Tsechansky & Yin), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22930 (published 2026-10).
