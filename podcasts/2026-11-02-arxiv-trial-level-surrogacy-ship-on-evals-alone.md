# Spotify — Can You Ship an LLM Change on Evals Alone?

A deep dive into when an offline eval can stand in for an A/B test on an LLM-powered product. Mårten Schultzberg argues the right question is trial-level surrogacy: do eval treatment effects predict online treatment effects across a class of comparable interventions, which is logically independent of whether the eval agrees with human judgment. We cover how sampling noise in launch logs attenuates the observed relationship and can be corrected, the resulting closed-form ship/test/kill rule, how LLM-judge accuracy that differs by arm can flip the sign of eval effects, why randomized audits are needed once a gate ships on evals alone, and an Upworthy headline illustration where the gate sends all 183 interventions to an A/B test. Source article: "When Can You Ship on Evals Alone? Trial-Level Surrogacy for Offline Evaluation of LLM Systems" — arXiv stat.ME (Mårten Schultzberg, Spotify), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10142 (published 2026-10).
