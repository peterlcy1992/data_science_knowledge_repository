# Independent research — Should You Recalibrate CTR Models on Fresh Data?

A deep dive into a temporal evaluation of post-hoc calibration for click-through-rate models on the Avazu log (40.4M impressions). We cover why random splits make a model look better calibrated than it will be on future traffic, why calibration error does not simply grow with distance from training, why recalibrating on a 6-hour slice of recent data increases error in about a third of applications while a 12-hour slice largely removes that harm, a size-matched control showing it is the time span rather than the impression count, and a simulated auction in which recalibration moved profit by only about a quarter of a percent either way. Source article: "How Much Fresh Data Should CTR Recalibration Use? A Temporal Evaluation of Post-hoc Calibration" — arXiv cs.LG (Rui Liu, independent researcher), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33168 (published 2026-10).
