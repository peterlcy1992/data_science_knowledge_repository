# Ashoka University — When covariates hide the pre-trend

Honest difference-in-differences methods judge how far parallel trends may fail after treatment by how far it failed before, using placebo estimates. This episode covers a new result: if the design adjusts for a covariate measured after the start of a placebo window, the covariate has already absorbed shocks to the outcome, so the placebos look flatter than the true trend and the confidence sets come out too narrow. We walk through the trend-plus-shock decomposition, a test and sensitivity tools for the problem, and the survey finding that 77 of 90 recent published studies adjusting for time-varying covariates date at least one covariate too late. Source article: "When Covariates Mask Pre-Trends" — arXiv econ.EM (Parush Arora, Ashoka University), https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03079 (published 2026-11).
