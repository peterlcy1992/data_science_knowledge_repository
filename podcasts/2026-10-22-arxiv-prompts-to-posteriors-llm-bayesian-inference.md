# LLM Agents — Can They Update Beliefs Like a Bayesian?

A deep dive into new work from Filbry, Melo and Gal asking whether LLM agents can perform Bayesian inference in context. LLMs are increasingly used to review literature, generate hypotheses and design experiments, all of which require revising beliefs as evidence arrives. The authors evaluate internal consistency and the ability to recover an externally determined posterior, using qualitative epistemological examples, quantitative Bayesian-inference benchmarks, and an analysis of how non-Bayesian updates hurt downstream tasks. Their finding: without tools, LLMs generally violate Bayes' rule when updating in context, but modern reasoning plus code execution improves performance substantially, even approximating analytically intractable posteriors. We also discuss what the abstract leaves unclear, such as the specific models and numbers. Source article: "From Prompts to Posteriors: Can LLM Agents Perform Bayesian Inference in Context?" — arXiv cs.AI (Filbry, Melo & Gal), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29331 (published 2026-10).
