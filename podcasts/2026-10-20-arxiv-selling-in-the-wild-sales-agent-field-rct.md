# Selling in the Wild — A Randomized Trial of an AI Sales Agent

A deep dive into a large randomized field trial of Javo, a sales agent fine-tuned on 4.6 million consented commercial conversations. About 14.8 million visitors across 100 live websites in 14 industries were randomized to no agent, Javo, or a frontier-model agent using the same interface, catalog tools and guardrails. Reported results: revenue per visitor up 10.0% versus no agent (95% CI +5.1 to +14.9), engaged-visitor conversion up 21.6% versus the frontier-model agent, and engaged conversion statistically non-inferior to human sales associates on 20 sites (11.26% vs 11.20%, one-sided p = 0.004) at 1.8% of the cost per conversation, with prespecified guardrails on returns, unsupported claims and complaints met. We also discuss what the abstract leaves unclear, such as how engaged visitors are defined. Source article: "Selling in the Wild: Benchmark and Randomized Field Evidence for a Sales Agent Trained on Commercial Conversations" — arXiv cs.IR (Tannenbaum), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25461 (published 2026-10).
