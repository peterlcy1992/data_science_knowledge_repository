# Uber — An AI First-Pass Reviewer for Product Requirement Docs

A deep dive into Uber's PRD Evaluator, an LLM-based first-pass reviewer for product requirement documents. We cover the business context (PRDs circulate through cross-functional checkpoints and often arrive missing context, so reviewers spend time on basic discovery), the workflow (assembling linked docs, prior experiments and Uber metric definitions; classifying the proposal to set review depth; scoring dimensions including Metric and Data Rigor; and producing a Ready / Ready with Caveats / Not Ready scorecard with a "start here" pointer), and the reported impact: early use by dozens of PMs and sharper, faster review discussions, with the caveat that the post gives no quantitative metrics and does not name the underlying model. Source article: "Lessons from Building a First-Pass AI PRD Reviewer at Uber" — Uber Blog (Lakshmi Ashok), https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/first-pass-prd/ (published 2026-05).
