# OneTrans — One Transformer for the Whole Recsys Cascade

A deep dive into OneTrans-V2, an industrial recommender that replaces the usual retrieval, pre-rank and fine-rank cascade with a single Transformer. We cover the business context (separate stage models that re-encode the same user history and are optimised in isolation), the technical approach (one shared encoding of the user's behaviour sequence, joint training with in-model distillation from fine-rank to pre-rank, sparse mixture-of-experts scaling with μP-style parameterisation, Decision-Conditioned Generative Retrieval and Sequence-Native Training), and the reported impact: +9.74% GMV in production and 3.2x the throughput of the separate per-stage models on the same hardware, with the caveat that the abstract gives no baseline or experiment details. Source article: "OneTrans-V2: Unifying Retrieval, Pre-rank, and Fine-rank with One Transformer in Industrial Recommender" — arXiv cs.IR (Cao et al.), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28589 (published 2026-09).
