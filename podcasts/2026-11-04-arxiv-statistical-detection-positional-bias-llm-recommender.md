# University of Granada — Do LLM Recommenders Pick by Position?

A deep dive into how to test whether an LLM-based product recommender is swayed by the order in which candidates appear in the prompt. We cover two statistical tests (a chi-square independence test on a contingency matrix and a permutation-based uniformity test that works under stochastic decoding), three metrics - permutation sensitivity, positional bias and strong positional bias - and a RAG testbed of 40,000 instances across eight open models in which every model rejects position independence. We also look at how the effect grows with candidate-set size and what lowering temperature does not fix, plus the audit steps the authors suggest such as order randomization. Source article: "Statistical Detection of Positional Bias in LLM-Based Recommender Systems: Methodologies and Empirical Evidence" — arXiv cs.IR (Peláez-González, Herrera, Herrera-Poyatos, University of Granada), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37962 (published 2026-10).
