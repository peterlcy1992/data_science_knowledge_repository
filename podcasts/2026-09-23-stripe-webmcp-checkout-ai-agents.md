**Stripe — Redesigning Checkout for AI Shopping Agents**

AI shopping agents can already complete a purchase through a checkout page — but until recently, they did it the hard way: parsing raw HTML, guessing which element was a button, and burning enormous amounts of time and tokens in the process. This episode digs into how Stripe rebuilt its checkout flow around WebMCP, a new browser standard that lets a webpage hand an AI agent a clean set of callable actions instead of a wall of HTML to interpret. We cover why the old approach was so expensive (nearly two million tokens and two and a half minutes for one purchase), how Stripe's design reveals only the actions that make sense at each step of checkout so the agent can't get confused or go off the rails, and why reusing the exact same backend that serves human shoppers turned out to matter as much as the new interface itself. Then we get into the numbers: a controlled test across six different AI models found the new approach cut token usage by more than 40%, shaved roughly a minute off every transaction, and still completed every single purchase successfully. Source article: "How Stripe Is Designing Checkout for AI Agents" — Stripe Dot Dev Blog, https://stripe.dev/blog/how-stripe-is-designing-checkout-for-ai-agents (published 2026-09).
