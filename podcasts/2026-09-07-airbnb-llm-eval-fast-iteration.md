# Airbnb — The LLM Eval Loop That Went From Weeks to a Day

A deep dive into how Airbnb rebuilt the evaluation loop for its production LLM systems so that a change could be verified in about a day instead of several weeks. We cover the business problem (evaluation noise so high that teams couldn't tell a genuine improvement from run-to-run variance, turning every iteration into a multi-week guessing game), the technical approach (splitting evaluation noise into aleatoric uncertainty — the task itself being ambiguous — versus epistemic uncertainty — an unreliable judge or model; deterministic per-sample caching of references and judge scores; small rank-under-50 LoRA "micro-adapters" trained in under an hour on a single GPU to patch behavior instead of retraining, with rules to fuse, retrain, or retire them; and full end-to-end validation to catch integration bugs component tests miss), and the realized payoff: same-day iteration on LLM system changes, unlocked almost entirely through classical software-engineering discipline rather than a bigger or better model.

Source article: "From Weeks to a Day: How We Made LLM Evaluation Fast Enough to Iterate On" — Airbnb Tech Blog, https://airbnb.tech/ai-ml/from-weeks-to-a-day-how-we-made-llm-evaluation-fast-enough-to-iterate-on/ (published 2026-07).
