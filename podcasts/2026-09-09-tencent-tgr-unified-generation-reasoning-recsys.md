# Tencent — Teaching a Recommender to Reason About Cold Starts

Tencent's recommenders serve hundreds of millions of users through the classic pipeline — retrieval, pre-ranking, ranking, reranking — with each stage optimized on its own. This episode digs into TGR, Tencent's push to bring a generative, LLM-style paradigm into that pipeline along three coupled fronts: a Transformer ranker that keeps the familiar per-item scoring shape while changing how it represents items internally, an end-to-end system that generates a ranked slate directly instead of scoring one item at a time, and — the most surprising piece — a way of injecting reasoning into recommendations without paying for it at serving time, by computing the reasoning offline and feeding the results in in during decoding. We walk through what problem each piece solves, why "just make it bigger" doesn't work for recommendation the way it does for chatbots, and the numbers Tencent reports from live traffic — including a nearly 5x jump in a cold-start metric, the kind of result recommendation teams chase for years.

Source article: "TGR: Advancing Industrial Recommendation from Generative-Paradigm Ranking toward Unified Generation and Reasoning" — Tencent / arXiv, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00986 (published 2026-09).
